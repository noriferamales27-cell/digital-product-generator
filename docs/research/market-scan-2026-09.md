# Market scan: digital products, September 2026

What is selling, at what price, on which platform, and where NorieDigi has a real edge. Numbers below come from the cited sources, not from NorieDigi sales. Treat them as direction, not forecast.

## What sells

| Category | Price band seen | Where it sells | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Notion and planner templates | $9 to $49 | Notion Marketplace, Gumroad, Etsy | Productivity systems, personal finance, content calendars, and lightweight CRMs carry roughly two thirds of paid template sales by volume |
| AI prompt packs | $27 to $97 per pack | Gumroad, Whop | Packs outperform single prompts by about 8x in total revenue even with fewer units |
| eBooks and guides on AI productivity | $4.99 to $24.99 | Gumroad, Amazon KDP, own site | Short, practical, job-specific guides beat general ones |
| AI-skills courses for non-technical professionals | $49 to $499 | Own site, Udemy, Gumroad | Highest ticket; highest production effort |
| Canva templates and printables | $5 to $29 | Etsy, Creative Market | Volume game; brand and preview quality decide |
| Business templates (proposals, SOPs, trackers) | $19 to $79 | Own site, Gumroad, Etsy | Buyers pay for "done for a real business," not blank layouts |

Seller earnings reported for Notion templates: $50 to $300 a month at 1 to 10 sales, $1,000 to $8,000 at 50 to 200 sales, five figures for creators with an audience or premium ($97+) business systems. The audience is the multiplier, not the template.

## What sells faster

- A pre-launch waitlist of 500+ converts at about 3x a cold launch, and 60% or more of launch-day traffic on winning launches comes from that list.
- The reported "minimum" paid launch budget of $15,000 to $20,000 is agency thinking. For a solo creator with an existing list, organic plus a small retargeting test is the realistic path. Ignore the budget, keep the waitlist rule.
- 8 to 12 weeks of warm-up is the agency timeline. With an existing list and content cadence, four weeks per product is enough.

## Competing generators

Inkfluence AI (eBooks, 33 blueprint types, cover, EPUB and audiobook export), Kupkaike (end-to-end product generation), Book Bolt (KDP low-content books), Canva AI (templates), Notion AI (Notion templates). Claude and ChatGPT remain the writing engines behind most of them.

Takeaway: do not build a generator to compete with these. Build a pipeline that uses them where useful and adds the thing they cannot: fifteen years of HR and operations work and a specific audience.

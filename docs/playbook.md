# How the winners sell digital products

What the people making real money from digital products actually do, and what the AED 7,000 "generator" offers are selling you. Numbers below come from the cited sources. Treat them as direction, not promises.

## The AED 7,000 offer, decoded

The offer in the video matches Iman Gadzhi's **Monetise** program: about USD 1,995 (roughly AED 7,300), sold through Whop. It bundles a course ("Digital Product Formula"), two AI tools (Synthesise AI, which picks a niche and outlines a product, and Ghostwriter OS, which writes sales copy), a "distribution software" that points you at communities where buyers already gather, a 6,000-member community, daily coaching calls, and a 90-day implementation contest. Refunds: a standard 7-day window. Reviews split the same way every time: buyers say the structure and community are the value, critics say the content exists free elsewhere and the marketing outruns the substance. Nobody has documented a lawsuit. He is, by every account, an excellent marketer first.

What it really sells: **a process plus accountability**, not a magic tool. The tool pieces are (1) niche research, (2) product outline and draft, (3) sales copy, and (4) a list of places the buyers are. This app does those four things on your own API key for a few dollars per product. What it does not replace is the community and the daily calls. Those you get from your own audience, or from a cheap peer group.

His funnel, which is the real lesson: every YouTube video is a "free training" that leads to a VSL and a checkout. Free value first, one clear next step, repeated daily. Copy the funnel shape, not the price.

## The five patterns every successful seller shares

**1. Distribution before product.** Thomas Frank made about USD 1,000,000 in 2022 from two Notion templates, with one product (Ultimate Brain) doing USD 760,000 of it. The templates sold because 2.5 million YouTube subscribers already trusted him. Easlo grew on X by giving away free Notion templates weekly, then sold premium ones and reached about USD 20,000 a month. Justin Welsh passed USD 500,000 in course sales on Gumroad in 22 months with no paid ads, from a daily LinkedIn habit. The product type matters less than owning one channel where your buyers already listen.

**2. The cheap product carries the revenue.** Roughly half of Easlo's revenue came from a USD 20 bullet journal and a USD 40 finance tracker, not his USD 130 flagship. Low-ticket products convert cold traffic and build the buyer list that later buys the expensive thing.

**3. Bundles and ladders beat single items.** Frank's bundle outsold his individual editions three to one and raised the average order. Prompt packs at USD 27 to 97 out-earn single prompts about eight to one on Gumroad and Whop. Dan Koe's revenue (about USD 2.5 million in 2023) came from a ladder: free writing, USD 150 course, USD 499 to 999 programs. Every product page should end with the next rung.

**4. Search is a channel, not just social.** Etsy printables sellers in the Gold City Ventures community report a combined USD 3.3 million in student sales; the repeated tactic is keyword research (KeySearch, Etsy search bar) to find a phrase where the existing listings do not answer the buyer's need, then making that exact product. One seller's top product came from a single keyword nobody was serving. Listing titles use the buyer's search words, not clever names.

**5. Margin is the whole point.** Justin Welsh runs at about 92% margin. Etsy digital downloads frequently exceed 90%. The cost is your time and the platform fee. So the platform choice is a margin decision, made below.

## How to sell: the mechanics that repeat

1. **Give the first piece away.** A free version of the product (three of the thirty prompts, the first checklist) goes to the community where buyers gather. The free piece captures an email.
2. **Pre-sell before you finish.** Welsh puts a product on Gumroad and a Carrd page in ten minutes and sells it before building the rest. If nobody buys the outline, do not write the book.
3. **Write for one buyer's search.** The listing title is the phrase they type. The first line of the description is their problem in their words.
4. **Launch in a window.** A 72-hour launch price, five emails, one post a day. Then a bundle at product three.
5. **Show the product in use.** Screen-share demos out-convert talking heads for tools and templates. Sixty to ninety seconds, the real task, no intro.
6. **Collect real proof.** One screenshot of a real result beats ten invented testimonials, and invented ones get stores banned.
7. **Ladder every buyer.** Free download, USD 15 to 39 product, USD 69 bundle, workshop, service. One next step on every page.

## How this app uses the playbook

- Step 1 searches marketplaces and scores opportunities on impact, confidence, and ease, so you build what already sells.
- Step 2 names the exact groups, hashtags, subreddits, and search terms where the buyers are, and the words they use.
- Step 3 writes the whole product in one pass so the pre-sell can become a delivery the same day.
- Step 4 prices in the tested bands, writes the listings in the buyer's search words, and produces the five-email launch and the 30-day calendar.
- Autopilot runs all four in one click for when you want a product in an hour.

# Launch playbook: sell faster

One product, one audience, one CTA. Business products go to SME owners on LinkedIn and Facebook business groups. Learning products go to OFWs and professionals on TikTok, Reels, and Facebook groups. Never in the same post or the same page section.

## The funnel

1. Free download (50 prompts, starter kit) captures the email into Supabase and HubSpot.
2. Waitlist page for the next product, opened four weeks before launch.
3. Launch week: five emails, twelve posts, two demo videos, founder price for 72 hours.
4. Buyer lands on the next rung: bundle, workshop, or strategy call.

## Four weeks before launch

- Waitlist block on /learning and a HubSpot form. Target 300 names; 500 is where conversion roughly triples.
- Two "pain mirror" posts and two "practical how-to" posts per week from the `content` skill, each showing one piece of the product in use.
- One screen-share demo video (60 to 90 seconds) of the product solving the real task.
- Ask five people from the audience to use a draft cold. Their words become the proof on the page. No invented testimonials.

## Launch week (day by day)

| Day | Email | Social | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "It's live" with the founder price and a real example inside | Demo video + one post | Founder price ends day 3 |
| 2 | The story behind it (why this product, what it fixed) | Pain mirror post | |
| 3 | Last hours of founder price, FAQ answered | Proof post with a buyer quote if one exists | Price rises at midnight |
| 5 | One thing buyers did with it this week | How-to post | Real result only |
| 7 | Quiet close: what is next and the bundle | Point of view post | Waitlist for the next product opens |

## After launch

- Bundle every product with its neighbour at product three.
- Affiliates: 30% to VA community leaders and workshop alumni, tracked through the checkout's affiliate feature.
- Marketplace copies on Gumroad and Etsy for search traffic, priced 10% higher than the own site so the site stays the best deal.
- Small retargeting test only after organic sales exist: $5 a day, warm audiences, seven days, stop if cost per sale exceeds the product price.
- Every buyer gets a three-email follow-up: how to use it, one tip, then the next rung.

## Measure

Weekly: waitlist size, launch-week sales, free-to-paid conversion, refund rate, buyer-to-call rate. Recorded in Supabase and read by the Sunday Learn routine.
